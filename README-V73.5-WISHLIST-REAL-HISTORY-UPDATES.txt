PricePulse V73.5 — Wishlist Real History Updates

- New Updates is limited to the customer's saved Wishlist products.
- Update status is driven by pp_retailer_price_history after the customer's last-seen timestamp.
- Same-price re-verification and 24h expiry alone do not create fake updates.
- Supports real best-price drop/increase, explicit stock changes, new retailer availability, and evidence-backed best-store changes.
- New Updates keeps normal Wishlist product cards and shows the latest real change in the card's left status area.
- Price Drops remains a dedicated filter and forces the saved-price drop label on those cards.
- Other site design and comparison modal are unchanged.
