PricePulse V73.4 — Wishlist Customer Updates

Changes only in Wishlist update logic:
- New Updates now shows normal Wishlist product cards, not a separate admin activity feed.
- It is limited to the customer’s saved Wishlist products.
- New Updates includes genuine changes since last seen: price drop, price increase, best-store change, stock/availability change, and retailer availability/count changes.
- Price drops remain available in the dedicated Price Drops filter and are also included in New Updates when newly changed.
- The left-side status on each product card shows the detected change.
- Same-price re-verification alone is not counted as a New Update.
- Existing design, comparison modal, product cards, and the rest of the site are unchanged.
